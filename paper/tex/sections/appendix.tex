\section{Reproducibility}
\label{app:repro}

Every figure and every number in this paper is produced by the scripts in \texttt{paper/scripts} of the \thecov\ repository, from reduced data products that record the NERSC runs they come from (\texttt{products/manifest.json}): \texttt{make products} reduces the raw bundles, \texttt{make figures numbers} rebuilds all figures and \texttt{tex/numbers.tex}, and \texttt{make paper} compiles the text. Each PDF figure carries in its metadata the script, the git commit and the hash of the products it read.
